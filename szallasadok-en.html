Intelligent AC Control

If a guest opens a window or balcony door, the air conditioner automatically stops after 3 minutes. This prevents the AC from running at 18°C next to open windows, protecting the equipment and keeping your bills low.

Automated Checkout Mode

The system syncs directly with your Airbnb, Booking, or other reservation calendars. Once the official checkout hour strikes (e.g., 10:00 AM), the entire property goes into eco-saver mode: turning off lights, TV, and setting heating/cooling to vacancy levels.

Smart Lock & PIN Entry

Forget physical key meetups and late-night emergencies. Every guest receives an automated gateway code that remains active exclusively from their designated check-in to checkout time, then self-deletes.

Flood Protection & Prevention

Compact, discrete sensors under washstands, laundry lines, and boilers. If water or a burst pipe is detected, the system immediately shuts off the main local water supply and alerts you instantly.

Noise Level Guard (Anti-Party)

100% GDPR-compliant decibel tracker that never records audio or private words. If a predefined noise threshold is exceeded at night, an automatic, polite SMS warning is fired directly to the guest, preventing local building disputes.

Remote Boiler & Heating

On vacancy days, the system does not spend energy heating empty rooms. Right before a new arrival, however, it automatically starts prep-warming the water and rooms based on your calendar times, offering a warm welcome.
