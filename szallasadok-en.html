Control & Savings Solutions for Landlords

My systems are custom-engineered for vacation rental owners. They help you regain absolute control of your property, cut utility spikes, and elevate the overall guest experience.

Intelligent A/C Interlock

If a guest leaves a window or terrace door open, the air conditioning system automatically shuts down after 3 minutes. This prevents running the AC at 18°C next to open windows, safeguarding your appliances and avoiding astronomical electricity bills.

Automated Check-out Mode

The apartment syncs directly with your Airbnb or Booking.com calendar. The exact minute check-out passes (e.g., 10:00 AM), the entire property goes into "Eco Mode": turning off left-on TVs, lighting, and adjusting heating or cooling until the next guest arrives.

Smart Lock & PIN Access

Eliminate physical handovers, lockboxes, and late-night check-ins. Guests receive a unique code active exclusively during the duration of their reserve (from check-in to check-out) that automatically deletes upon departure.

Leak Detection & Isolation

Discreet smart moisture sensors placed under washing machines, dishwashers, and water boilers. If a threshold leak is registered, the central main valve shuts off water instantly, notifying your phone and shielding you from major insurance damage.

Noise-Monitoring (Party Prevention)

100% GDPR-compliant noise level tracking. It does NOT record speech or audio. If levels exceed critical thresholds at night, an immediate automated SMS warns guests, saving you from residential blocks and housing committee complaints.

Intelligent Boiler & Heating Control

On days with no incoming guests, water boilers and space heaters run at minimum levels. Hours prior to guest arrivals, the booking calendar automatically signals the boiler and HVAC to prepare a perfectly comfortable environment.
