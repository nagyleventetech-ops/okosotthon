⚠️ 230V Mains / Electrician Cooperation Rules

HelyiOkos prioritizes safety above all else. While the bulk of our sensors and switches operate off harmless low-volt button batteries (incorporating no dust, drills, or cables), in-wall relays, floor thermostats, and heavy garage openers run on standard 230V high-voltage lines.

❗ For your safety and warranty verification, 230V mains high-voltage electrical mounts must only be conducted by a licensed, certified electrician.

What your electrician does:

Physically fits süllyesztett (in-wall) modules behind switches, wires 230V cables on boiler terminals, and hooks mains power to smart assets.

What HelyiOkos does:

I construct complete schematic diagrams and assist your contractor. Once hooked up, I carry out the entire software config, offline central key pairings, and custom routines.
